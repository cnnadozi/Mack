Mack setup

Enter your own temporary Gemini API key (from Google AI Studio). Only Mack's background worker reads it; web pages never see it.

The key stays in this browser session. Enter it again after restarting Chrome or reloading Mack.

After saving, open any https website (for example uhc.com) and click Mack in the browser toolbar. Mack stays on in that tab, including on sign-in pages at other domains, until you press Exit or close the tab.
